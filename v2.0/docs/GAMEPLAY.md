## 📊 Game Mechanics

### Scoring System

**Base Formula**: `10 points × Difficulty Multiplier × Power-up Multiplier`

#### Score Calculation Examples

| Difficulty | Power-up Active | Points per Food   |
| ---------- | --------------- | ----------------- |
| Easy       | No              | 10 (10 × 1)       |
| Medium     | No              | 15 (10 × 1.5)     |
| Hard       | No              | 20 (10 × 2)       |
| Easy       | Yes (2×)        | 20 (10 × 1 × 2)   |
| Medium     | Yes (2×)        | 30 (10 × 1.5 × 2) |
| Hard       | Yes (2×)        | 40 (10 × 2 × 2)   |

**Pro Tip**: Collect Score Multiplier power-ups on Hard difficulty for maximum points!

## 🎯 Strategy Guide

### Beginner Strategies (Easy Difficulty)

1. **Stay Near Edges**: Keep your snake close to the walls in early game
2. **Plan Two Moves Ahead**: Think about where your tail will be
3. **Avoid Center Initially**: Don't venture into the middle until your snake is longer
4. **Power-up Priority**: Focus on Speed Boost first to learn controls

### Intermediate Strategies (Medium Difficulty)

1. **Spiral Pattern**: Move in spirals from outside to inside as you grow
2. **Corner Traps**: Use corners strategically when your snake is large
3. **Power-up Timing**: Save Invincibility for when space gets tight
4. **Speed Control**: Use Speed Boost in open areas, avoid in tight spaces

### Advanced Strategies (Hard Difficulty)

1. **Pre-planning Routes**: Plan your path 5-10 moves in advance
2. **Risk Management**: Calculate risk vs reward before collecting power-ups
3. **Efficiency Moves**: Minimize wasted movement
4. **Invincibility Exploitation**: Use Invincibility to create shortcuts through your body
5. **Score Multiplier Stacking**: Activate Score Multiplier before collecting multiple foods

## 🤖 AI Opponent Strategies

### Understanding AI Behaviour

The AI uses **Breadth-First Search (BFS)** pathfinding:

- Targets closest food when hungry
- Chases power-ups when available
- Avoids collisions with walls and bodies
- Makes optimal moves based on shortest path

### Beating the AI

1. **Cut Off Food**: Position yourself between the AI and food
2. **Power-up Racing**: Rush to power-ups faster than the AI
3. **Defensive Play**: Block the AI's path without colliding
4. **Use Speed Boost**: Outpace the AI to food when you have speed boost
5. **Invincibility Advantage**: Pass through your body to reach food faster

### AI Weaknesses

- AI sometimes takes longer paths when confused
- AI prioritises food over safety in desperate situations
- AI doesn't predict your future moves

## 💎 Power-up Mastery

### Speed Boost (Yellow) - 5 seconds

**Best Uses**:

- Early game to collect more food quickly
- Racing the AI to food or power-ups
- When you have plenty of open space

**Avoid When**:

- Snake is very long (risk of collision)
- In tight spaces or corners
- Low on space and reflexes

### Invincibility (Cyan) - 7 seconds

**Best Uses**:

- Late game when space is limited
- Creating shortcuts through your own body
- Desperate situations when trapped
- Blocking the AI without risk

**Pro Strategies**:

- Use to collect multiple foods in congested areas
- Combine with Speed Boost for ultimate efficiency
- Position yourself strategically before it expires

### Score Multiplier (Purple) - 10 seconds

**Best Uses**:

- When multiple foods are nearby
- Combined with Hard difficulty (2× base score)
- Middle to late game when you can collect multiple foods

**Maximize Value**:

- Plan a route to collect 3-4 foods during duration
- Don't activate in early game with few opportunities
- Combine with Speed Boost for maximum collection

## 📈 Progression Tips

### Early Game (0-50 points)

- Focus on learning controls
- Stay near edges
- Collect all power-ups to practice
- Don't rush

### Mid Game (50-150 points)

- Start using spiral patterns
- Time your power-ups wisely
- In AI mode, start competing aggressively
- Plan 3-5 moves ahead

### Late Game (150+ points)

- Maximum precision required
- Use Invincibility strategically
- Every move counts
- In AI mode, defensive play if you're ahead
